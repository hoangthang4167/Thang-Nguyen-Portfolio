About Me

I am Thang Nguyen

Full-stack software engineer building complete products end to end — responsive, high-performance frontends with React, Next.js, and TypeScript, paired with backends in NestJS and Go on PostgreSQL. I've shipped platforms across fintech, government revenue collection, ed-tech, real estate, and responsible-gambling compliance, owning everything from UI/UX and API design to deployment. Strong attention to detail, clean and scalable code, and a proven track record of delivering reliable, high-quality projects on time.

Backend Development

Full-Stack Product Development
